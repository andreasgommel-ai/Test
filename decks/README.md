# AI's Impact on the Future of Careers in Europe — Mercer (Internal)

Internal leadership briefing deck on how AI is reshaping European careers, with
strategic implications for Mercer's **Career** consulting business.

- **Audience:** Mercer Career — Europe leadership (internal)
- **Focus:** Blended — Mercer's market POV on AI + careers in Europe *and* the
  implications for Mercer's own Career offerings
- **Output:** `Mercer_AI_Career_Europe.pptx` (16:9, 13 slides)

## Slide flow

1. Title
2. Agenda
3. Executive summary
4. The AI inflection point (macro context)
5. The talent paradox (Mercer Global Talent Trends 2026)
6. Automation vs. augmentation in Europe
7. The widening skills gap (with chart)
8. Workforce sentiment & trust
9. Implications for Mercer Career — opportunity vs. pressure
10. Mercer's starting position (existing assets)
11. Strategic recommendations (Now / Next / Later)
12. Closing — the opportunity
13. Appendix — sources & methodology
