# AI's Impact on Careers in Europe — Research Notes & Sources

Compiled for the Mercer-branded executive deck. All figures cross-checked against
primary-source organizations. Where a primary PDF blocked automated fetching (HTTP
403), figures were corroborated via primary-document search extracts and consistent
secondary citations — flagged as such.

## 1. Scale of AI exposure (Europe)
- **IMF (Jan 2024):** ~**60% of jobs in advanced economies** (incl. Western Europe) are exposed to AI; roughly **half** of those could be negatively affected, half could benefit from productivity gains. Global ~40%. — IMF Staff Discussion Note SDNEA2024001.
- **OECD:** ~**27–28% of jobs** across OECD are in occupations at highest risk of automation. — OECD Employment Outlook / AI & Work.
- **Goldman Sachs (2023, baseline):** ~**two-thirds of jobs in the US and Europe** exposed to some AI automation; in Europe **clerical roles ~45%** of tasks automatable vs **~4%** in crafts/trades.

## 2. Jobs created vs displaced
- **WEF Future of Jobs 2025 — Europe:** AI/information-processing tech expected to **create ~11M and displace ~9M jobs in Europe** — largest impact of any tech category in the region.
- **WEF global:** 170M created, 92M displaced by 2030 → net **+78M (+7%)**; **22% structural churn**.
- **McKinsey (2024):** up to **~12M occupational transitions** needed in Europe by 2030; **~27% of current work hours** in Europe could be automated by 2030.

## 3. Productivity upside
- **McKinsey:** Europe could reach up to **~3% annual productivity growth** to 2030 with fast adoption + redeployment (vs ~0.3% if slow).
- **PwC Global AI Jobs Barometer 2025:** productivity growth in most AI-exposed industries **~4x** higher (7%→27%); **56% wage premium** for AI-skilled roles; skills sought changing **66% faster**.

## 4. Skills shift & reskilling
- **WEF 2025:** **39% of workers' core skills** transformed/outdated by 2025–2030 (global). Analytical thinking essential for **70%** of employers; AI & big data the fastest-growing skill.
- **Cedefop (EU, 2024 survey):** **~6 in 10 EU workers** susceptible to AI-related task transformation; **28%** already interact with AI at work. **61%** expect to need new skills; **44%** doubt employer will provide adequate training; **15%** fear AI job loss within 5 years.
- **Eurostat / Digital Decade:** only **56% of EU adults** have basic digital skills (target 80% by 2030); EU **~9.7M ICT specialists short** of its 2030 target.
- **LinkedIn:** EU professionals adding AI-literacy skills grew **80x** (2022→2023); "AI engineer" among fastest-growing roles.

## 5. Mercer Global Talent Trends (2024–2025)
- **65% of executives** expect **11–30% of their workforce** to be reskilled/redeployed due to AI within two years.
- **63% of employees** would trade a 10% pay raise for guaranteed AI/digital upskilling & career development.
- **98% of companies** planning organizational design changes.
- ~**58%** using AI to augment/replace tasks; Mercer's framing: **"redesign work, not just jobs"** (decompose jobs into tasks; human + AI augmentation).
- HR readiness gap: only ~**a third of HR leaders** report a clear AI strategy/roadmap.
- Strategic themes: **skills-based organizations**, **human + AI augmentation**, **trust & transparency** in talent decisions, the **"good work" agenda**.

## Verification caveats
- WEF/IMF/Mercer/Cedefop primary PDFs frequently returned HTTP 403; figures confirmed via primary-document extracts + consistent restatements. For a citation-grade final, confirm exact wording against source PDFs.
- WEF "39%" skills and LinkedIn "70%" figures are **global**; cleanest Europe-specific numbers come from **Cedefop** and **Eurostat**.
- Goldman Sachs baseline figures date from 2023 but remain widely referenced.

## Addendum (Sep 2026): country deep-dives and exposure calculator

Added for the interactive dashboard (`dashboard.html`). Primary hosts frequently blocked automated fetching; figures are corroborated via document extracts and official restatements. Items marked *verify* rest on secondary reporting.

### Germany
- Enterprises using AI 26% (2025), 20% (2024) — Destatis IKT-Erhebung. GenAI in ~25% of establishments (5% in 2023), training in ~1 in 4 AI-using firms — IAB-Kurzbericht 8/2026. ifo 41% (2025) → 55% (May 2026); Bitkom 36%.
- AI-touched postings 4.2% (Indeed Hiring Lab Q1 2026); ~147k AI postings 2024 (PwC); AI skills penetration 1.9, 3rd globally (Stanford AI Index 2025).
- 8 in 10 use AI tools; 70% expect AI job cuts; 36% worried about own job (EY European AI Barometer 2025). Cedefop DE worker-AI share conflicting in secondary sources (25–43%) — *verify*.
- 38% of employees (13.0M) in high-substitutability occupations, 2022 — IAB 6/2025. 79% of firms lack AI competencies — Stifterverband/McKinsey 2025.
- Law: §87(1) No. 6 BetrVG (BAG 1 ABR 20/21, MS 365); §90, §80(3) BetrVG; ArbG Hamburg 24 BVGa 1/24 (ChatGPT private accounts); KI-MIG in force 29 Jul 2026, Bundesnetzagentur market surveillance.
- Policy: Qualifizierungschancengesetz / Qualifizierungsgeld; Weiterbildungsoffensive 2030 (65% participation target); only 45% of establishments supported training H1 2025 (IAB-Betriebspanel).

### France
- Enterprises using AI 18% (2025), 10% (2024); 58% for 250+; 21% of employees AI-trained — INSEE Première 2120/2061.
- >166k AI postings 2024, #1 in Europe, +273% since 2018 (PwC); 3.3% AI-touched postings (Indeed).
- ~1 in 4 use AI daily; 66% positive (EY 2025). Ifop/Talan 2025: 9% have employer GenAI tools, 15% trained, 73% feel unprepared.
- ~16% of tasks exposed, up to 5M jobs concerned — Coface/OEM 2025. Banking ~12% of jobs to evolve — FBF 2025. France Stratégie/Trésor figures not retrieved.
- Law: Art. L.2312-8 Code du travail; TJ Créteil 15 Jul 2025 (RG 25/00851); TJ Paris 2 Sep 2025 (RG 25/53278); CNIL recruitment guidance; 2026 inspection priority — *verify*.
- Policy: Osez l'IA (€200m; 80% SMEs by 2030; 15M trained); CPF 1.4M trained 2024. Mercer FR 51% investing in AI — *verify*.

### United Kingdom
- 29% of businesses use AI (Jun 2026, +8 pp; 49% of large) — ONS. DSIT lower-definition 16%.
- 180k specialist AI postings 2025 (+61%); AI wage premium 34% (PwC UK 2026). Indeed 2.7% — *verify*.
- 49% of workers used AI for their job; 14% untrained; 18% see nothing in AI for their role; <25% feel fairly paid — Mercer HR Technology's Impact on the Workforce 2025. CIPD: AI used in 76% of orgs; 1 in 6 employers expect headcount cuts.
- IPPR 2024: 11% of tasks now → ~59%; central 4.4M jobs lost / +6.3% GDP; worst 7.9M; best 0. DfE 2023: 10–30% highly automatable. TBI 2024: 1–3M displaced by 2050.
- Law: no AI Act; Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.80 (Feb 2026); Employment Rights Act 2025 (no AI provisions); ICO recruitment guidance; TUC draft bill 2024; 60% believe monitored.
- Policy: TechFirst £187m; 10M workers AI skills by 2030; Skills England benchmark 2026; 97% of AI firms report a skills gap (DSIT).

### Nordics
- Eurostat 2025: DK 42.0%, FI 37.8%, SE 35.0%, NO 28.9%. Statistics Denmark 2026: 59%. Statistics Finland 38% (80% ICT). SCB 35%.
- PwC 2025: DK 2.9% of postings AI roles. Solita/Kantar 2025: weekly GenAI use DK 65%, FI 62%. Tieto 2026: 39% of Swedish employees use AI extensively. EY 2026: used AI FI 85%, DK 83%, NO 82%, SE 78%; 61% of Nordic firms provide AI training.
- IMF SIP 25/119: ~1/5 of Danish workforce at displacement risk. Danmarks Nationalbank 2026: AI adopters show lower employment growth, no aggregate effect yet. Only 45% of Nordic employers expect major automation vs 65% global (2026 exec survey).
- Law: EU AI Act (NO via EEA pending); Danish Industrial Agreement 2025–28; 3F–Hilfr AI agreement; Unionen 67% of workplaces introduced AI; 6 in 10 white-collar lack rules (Akavia).
- Policy: Elements of AI >1M learners; Sweden AI Commission 75 measures; Denmark DKK 62.5m strategy 2024–27.

### Exposure calculator method
- Occupation base: Goldman Sachs (Mar 2023) share of tasks automatable by GenAI by SOC group (Europe 24% vs US 25%). Values for office/admin 46, legal 44, engineering 37, business/finance 35, management 32, sales 31, construction 6, maintenance 4 are corroborated; remaining rows follow the report's ranking and should be checked against its exhibit before external use.
- Industry tier: PwC 2025 exposure tiers (high: financial services, ICT/software, professional services; low: mining, hospitality, construction). Applied as ±10% nudge. Cited sector context: McKinsey 2023 GenAI value by industry (verified: banking 2.8–4.7%, high tech up to 9%, pharma up to 5%, education up to 4%, retail 1.2–2.0%); McKinsey Europe 2024 hours automatable 18% (healthcare) – 30% (manufacturing); WEF 2025 financial services automated task share 21% → 38%.
- Augmentation share: indicative, from IMF SDN 2024 (advanced economies 33% high-exposure/low-complementarity vs 27% high-complementarity), ILO WP 96/140 (clerical highest automation gradient), Goldman 2025 and McKinsey 2024 augmentation evidence.
- The calculator is a directional prioritisation aid, not a job-level assessment; metrics across sources are not averaged.
